What your BMI result means

A BMI result can help you understand whether your weight is in a healthy range for your height. It is one useful measure, but it doesn't tell the whole story. Factors like muscle mass, age, and other aspects of your health matter too.

✦

Healthy eating

Make balanced food choices with a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and protein sources to support your overall health.

↗

Regular exercise

Find movement you enjoy. Regular physical activity can support heart health, strength, energy, and general well-being.

☾

Adequate sleep

Consistent, restful sleep helps your body recover and supports your mood, concentration, and overall health.

Limitations of BMI

BMI is a helpful screening tool, but it should not be used on its own to diagnose health conditions. Consider these additional factors when assessing health.

♙

Gender

BMI does not directly account for differences in body composition or how fat is distributed between individuals.

◷

Age

Age can influence body composition. BMI is interpreted differently for children and teens than for adults.

⌁

Muscle

People with more muscle may have a higher BMI even when they have a healthy level of body fat.

♡

Pregnancy

BMI is not intended to assess weight status during pregnancy. Ask a healthcare professional for guidance.

◎

Race and ethnicity

Health risks can vary between populations, so BMI thresholds may not reflect every individual's risk equally.
